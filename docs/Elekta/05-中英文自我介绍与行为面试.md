# Elekta｜中英文自我介绍与行为面试

短句表达，先练结构再录音。依据现有个人材料；求职动机必须符合本人真实意愿。JD未写AI职责，不以未确认的AI机会作为加入理由。

## 1. 60–90秒自我介绍

**中文**

面试官您好，我叫沈瑊玏，本科是华东师范大学通信工程，硕士是南洋理工大学计算机。我的主要经验是Java后端，也做过前端和个人Agent项目。

在携程实习期间，我参与订单附加产品开发、旧接口迁移和退款并发问题处理。在招行信用卡中心，我做过补件配置管理站点、前端性能优化和内部知识检索课题。

这次面试我希望重点介绍两类能力：一类是在已有系统上理解规则、完成开发；另一类是围绕兼容性和异常路径定位问题、控制变更影响。Elekta这个岗位涉及MOSAIQ长期开发维护和全球团队协作，与这些经历有连接。我也愿意补齐C#/.NET及医疗软件质量流程知识。

## 2. Why Elekta？

**中文：**按这份JD，岗位既做新功能，也要跟进真实客户问题、兼容性和质量验证。我希望积累对一个产品长期负责的经验。医疗场景让我更关注软件行为是否准确、变更能否被验证；我的接口迁移和问题定位经验可以迁移，但临床业务和质量流程需要认真学习。

> What interests me is the combination of product development and long-term maintenance. From the job description, engineers also investigate customer issues and work with quality teams. My API migration and debugging experience is relevant to that work. I would also like to learn how software is developed and validated in a clinical setting.

这是可选表达，不替用户虚构“从小热爱医疗”的经历。公司产品信息见07。

## 3. 你之前想做AI，为什么现在考虑这个岗位？

**回答思路：**保留真实经历和离职原因，解释目前选择标准；不要将旧答案中的“Tesla/Agent”简单替换为Elekta。

若你确实接受以传统软件为主，可说：

> I have spent time exploring AI engineering, and I still find it interesting. But I also value core software work: understanding requirements, handling failures, and maintaining a product over time. I understand that this role is focused on MOSAIQ development and maintenance. I'm considering it on that basis, rather than assuming that it is an AI role.

如果AI工作仍是必要条件，应坦诚确认岗位是否匹配，不能为拿offer承诺不符合自己的职责安排。

## 4. 为什么离开招行？为什么任职时间短？

现有材料记录你希望更多投入AI工程。可以说明当时的判断以及现在对长期匹配的重视，不改写为“为了进入医疗行业离职”。

> It was a short experience, and I understand the concern. At the time, I wanted to explore AI engineering more deeply. Since leaving, I have continued working on personal projects. That experience has also made me more careful about role fit. This time, I want to understand the daily work, the team's expectations, and the opportunities for long-term growth.

**追问会不会再次离职：**用本次会核实什么回答，不给空泛保证。

> I would like to align on the actual responsibilities and technical environment before joining. I am looking for a role where I can keep improving a product and build deeper engineering experience.

## 5. Java转C#，多久能上手？

> My production experience is mainly in Java. The transferable parts are APIs, databases, business rules, and debugging. I still need to learn the C# ecosystem and the codebase. I would start with a small, well-defined change, add tests, and learn through code review. I would agree on concrete milestones with the team instead of promising a fixed number of days.

谈已学内容时按事实使用“I have reviewed…”或“I plan to study…”。没有完成Demo就不说已经实现。

## 6. 英文项目一：API迁移

> At Ctrip, I helped retire about one hundred inactive APIs and migrate about ten active read APIs. The main challenge was preserving behavior for existing callers.
>
> We checked usage before retiring APIs. For active read APIs, we compared old and new responses and switched traffic gradually with a rollback option. The two services shared a database, so this was not a database migration.
>
> I learned to check more than successful responses. Default values, ordering, and error behavior also matter.

**追问：What would you improve?**

> I would make the compatibility cases more explicit and keep a reusable regression set. I would also document which differences are acceptable and why.

改进用would；不要将建议测试集写成当年已完成。

## 7. 英文项目二：退款锁竞争

> Each refund record still produced its own message. The consumer could process the current refund together with other compatible records.
> Different messages could produce different orders for the same group. Each task could hold part of the locks and fail to get the rest.
> My main change was to sort the full group by refund ID before taking the first lock.
> Unfinished records could be sent again by a scheduled scan. Unsubmitted records past the time limit could use the single-refund fallback.
> Consistent ordering addresses the lock conflict. Duplicate effects still need stable refund identities and a downstream contract.

**追问：How would you reproduce it?**

> I would start from two different current refund IDs and let each worker build its group.
> In the old flow, I would control their timing after the first locks. In the fixed flow, I would check that both compete for the same first lock.

验证用would；“当前＋其他、当前置顶”是最新材料选定还原，不是新增源码证据。不能先锁当前记录再排序。完整追问见[11](11-简历技术追问英文卡.md)。

## 9. 跨国协作与书面更新

**口头回答：**

> I keep updates clear: the observed problem, the evidence, what is still unknown, and the next step. I confirm time zones and deadlines. If I don't understand a requirement, I restate it and ask for an example.

**模拟缺陷更新，不是真实事件：**

> Subject: Upgrade issue — reproduction status
>
> We reproduced the missing-record issue in the test environment using the reported version and configuration. The records are present in the database, but the new filter excludes one status value. We are checking whether other screens use the same filter. Next, we will add a regression case and review the proposed fix with QA. The full impact is still under investigation.

## 10. 行为题不编造事件

协作题可以讲EasyAccess供应商联调；具体冲突、个人失误、说服同事的故事需补真实事件。按“分歧是什么—我提供什么证据—团队如何决定—结果与反思”准备，不杜撰对话和功劳。

**没听清：**Could you repeat the last part of the question?

**确认范围：**Would you like me to focus on the design or my own contribution?

**不确定：**I don't remember the exact detail, so I don't want to guess.

**未实现：**I haven't implemented that part. My initial approach would be…

**AI辅助开发：**I use AI tools to explore and draft code, but I am responsible for reviewing and testing it. In a company environment, I would follow the rules for approved tools and data access.
